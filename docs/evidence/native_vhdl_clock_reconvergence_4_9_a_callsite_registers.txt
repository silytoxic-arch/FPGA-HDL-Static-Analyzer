The diagnostic branch requires matching_leaf_count >= 2. The final pass thus
counts identifiers only against the second set. There is no loop that merges
rbp-0x48 back into rbp-0x68, so construction is one hop rather than transitive.
A post-convergence alias has an identifier RHS and also fails the separate
final-root mask; it cannot promote the earlier expression into a candidate.
