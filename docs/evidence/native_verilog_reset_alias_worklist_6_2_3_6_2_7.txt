Conclusion used by the product
------------------------------
The native rule performs transitive, visited-name-deduplicated traversal of
direct and single-NOT continuous reset aliases. A chain such as
`async_rst <- alias_rst <- (A & B)` reaches and reports the upstream non-alias
driver. A direct/inverted alias cycle terminates without a diagnostic proof.

Diagnostic multiplicity
-----------------------
The visited tree is consulted only before a source name is inserted into the
next work set. Inside one current-name iteration, FUN_180758410 loops over every
matching assignment record; each non-alias record independently executes the
diagnostic construction and append calls. There is no name- or line-based
deduplication in that block. Consequently two distinct non-alias assignment
AST nodes report twice even on one physical line, while two alias branches that
converge on one source name visit that source once.
