Candidate predicate in FUN_1805e5770
------------------------------------
The helper always recurses through every child at the end of the function. It
is not restricted to roots of type 0x75/0x54/0xa3/0xa4.

For a current AST_ARCHITECTURE, AST_BLOCK, AST_GENERIC or AST_PORT node, every
direct child whose type is AST_SIGNAL, AST_VARIABLE, AST_WIRE, AST_LOCALPARAM
or AST_IDENTIFIER is compared with the configured minimum/maximum and appended
to the too-short or too-long vector.

The current node itself is a candidate when it is AST_ARCHITECTURE, AST_ENTITY,
AST_TYPE, AST_PROCEDURE, AST_SUBTYPE or AST_FUNCTION and has a nonempty name.
A named AST_ALWAYS is also a candidate unless its name is exactly `process` or
`PROCESS`.

AST_BLOCK has an additional native gate: its stored name must be longer than
0x16 (22) bytes and must not begin with the internal parser container names
`enterProcess_statement` or `enterSequential_statement`. Consequently a source
block label of 21 or 22 bytes is excluded even though the global maximum is 20;
a non-synthetic 23-byte block label is reported.
